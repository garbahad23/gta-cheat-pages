Cheat Vault

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what data the app uses. If you have a question, write to support@example.com.

Summary

  • You don't need an account, and we don't run servers that store your data.
  • Your unlocked cheats, favorites and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • In the free version, Google AdMob shows the ads you choose to watch. Google may collect device data for this.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. RevenueCat checks and restores them.
  • We don't use analytics, and we don't sell personal data.

Data stored on your phone

The app keeps a local database on your phone. It contains the cheat codes, the cheats you have unlocked, your favorites, your language, game and platform choices, and whether you have bought the full unlock. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob, which you choose to watch to unlock a cheat. So that an ad is ready when you want it, the app loads one in the background. When you buy the full unlock, the app doesn't load ads.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device and advertising identifiers (on iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device type, operating system and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crashes.

Google uses this data to show ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and, where you agree to it, show personalized ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, Apple asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not used.

Read more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The one-time "Unlock All Cheat Codes" purchase is paid through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details.

The app uses RevenueCat to check purchases and restore them on a new phone. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app, the details of your purchase (product, date, transaction ID, store country), and basic device information such as the app version and operating system. Read the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law of your country. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the Turkish KVKK or Californian law), you can ask to access or delete your personal data. We don't hold data about you on our own servers.

  • To delete the data on your phone, delete the app.
  • To change how ads use your data, use Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app, or your phone's privacy settings.
  • To ask about purchase data held by RevenueCat, write to support@example.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
