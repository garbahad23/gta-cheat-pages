Write to support@example.com. Please include your phone model, the game, your platform and the name of the cheat.

A cheat doesn't work

  • Enter the code during gameplay, not in a menu or on the map. On console, press the buttons quickly and in the exact order.
  • Check that the platform in the app (PlayStation, Xbox or PC) matches yours.
  • Some cheats only exist in one edition, for example the Definitive Edition of GTA San Andreas or the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S version of GTA 5. The cheat's note in the app says so.
  • In Red Dead Redemption 2, type the phrase exactly as shown in Settings → Cheats → Enter cheat. Some cheats need to be found in a newspaper first.
  • In The Sims 4, enter "testingcheats true" first.

Do cheats turn off trophies and achievements?

Yes, in most games, while the cheat is active. Keep a save without cheats.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account that you used to buy, then tap Settings → Restore purchases in the app.

The ad doesn't load

Ads need an internet connection, and sometimes no ad is available. Try again a little later. The cheat codes themselves work offline.

My unlocked cheats are gone after I reinstalled the app

Cheats unlocked with ads are stored only on your phone and are deleted with the app. The one-time purchase can always be restored.

Are GTA 6 cheats included?

Not yet. There are no confirmed GTA 6 cheat codes. We'll add them in an update as soon as they are found.

How do I change the language or my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings in the app. The app uses your phone's language by default.
